## You are in control

Bursa's official Privacy Center at **privacy.bursanalar.com** is operated internally by **PT Global Makmur Madani**. This is not a decorative legal page - you can read policies **and** submit data-subject requests under the PDP Law.

## Make a request

1. Click **Make a privacy request** on any policy page or the hub.
2. Choose your subject type (account holder, waitlist/non-account, mentor applicant).
3. Pick a request type (access, correction, erasure, etc.).
4. Submit the form - you will receive a **reference number** by email.

Erasure, portability, or consent withdrawal requires **sign-in** with the same email.

## Track status

Open [Track request](/en/requests/status), enter email + reference number, verify with a 6-digit OTP.

## Limitations

- Manual processing by our privacy team (not enterprise automation).
- Legally required data (e.g. tax transaction records) may not be erasable - we explain in writing.
- SLA: **within 14 business days**.

## Cookie preferences

Manage cookies on [Cookie Policy](/en/cookies#manage) - essential, functional, and analytics choices are recorded in our systems.
