Under UU PDP, you have rights over your personal data. Use this page to submit a request.

## Request types

| Type | Description |
|-------|-----------|
| **Data access** | Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold |
| **Correction** | Fix inaccurate data |
| **Erasure** | Delete the account and personal data |
| **Withdraw consent** | Revoke consent for non-essential processing |
| **Objection** | Object to certain processing |
| **Portability** | Ask for data in a readable format (JSON) |

## How to submit

Fill in the form below or email [privacy@bursanalar.com](mailto:privacy@bursanalar.com) with:

1. **Full name** as on the account
2. **Email** registered at Bursa
3. **Request type** (from the table above)
4. **Request detail** — be specific about what you need
5. **Identity verification** — we may ask for extra confirmation to protect your account

## Response time

We respond within **14 working days**. Complex requests may take longer — we will tell you.

## Exceptions

Some data **cannot be deleted** because of legal duties (for example transaction history for tax reporting). In that case we will explain the reason in writing.

## Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the data-protection authority under UU PDP.
