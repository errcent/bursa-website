This Cookie Policy supplements the [Privacy Policy](/en/policies) and explains how Bursa uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help the platform function, remember your preferences, and (if you agree) help us understand aggregate usage.

## Types of cookies we use

### Essential cookies (required)

| Cookie | Function | Duration |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Authentication session | Keep you logged in | Session / 30 days |
| CSRF token | Form security | Session |
| Theme preference | Light/dark mode | 1 year |

Essential cookies **cannot be turned off** because they are required for the platform to work.

### Functional cookies

| Cookie | Function | Duration |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Learning progress (local) | Temporary video position | Local persistent |
| Language preference | Display language | 1 year |

### Analytics cookies (optional)

| Cookie | Provider | Function |
|--------|----------|--------|
| `_ph_*` | PostHog (planned) | Aggregate product analytics |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics (planned) | Traffic & funnel |

Analytics cookies are active only if you consent.

### Marketing cookies (optional)

Bursa currently **does not use** marketing/retargeting cookies. If they are enabled in the future, we will ask for separate consent.

## Local storage

In addition to cookies, we use local storage for:

- Display preferences (theme)
- Non-sensitive cache for performance

Sensitive data (authentication tokens) is stored with appropriate security controls.

## How to manage cookies

1. **Consent banner** — set preferences on first visit (Phase 2).
2. **Browser settings** — block/delete cookies via Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.
3. **Google OAuth** — revoke third-party access via [Google Account Security](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Disabling essential cookies can affect login and checkout.

## Changes

We may update this cookie policy. Material changes will be notified via the platform.
