_Effective: 22 July 2026_

**If the Indonesian and English versions conflict, the Indonesian version governs.**

**Does Bursa sell my data?** No. See [Subprocessors](/en/subprocessors).

**Can mentors or admins read my Notes?** No. Notes are private.

**Do you store card numbers?** No. Gateways do.

**How do I delete my account?** [Data requests](/en/requests) or account settings. Some tax records may be retained anonymised.

**Cookies?** Essential plus optional analytics with consent. [Cookie policy](/en/cookies).

Contact: privacy@bursanalar.com (14 working days).
