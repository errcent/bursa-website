_Effective: 22 July 2026_

If the Indonesian and English versions differ, **the Indonesian version governs.**

## Does Bursa sell my personal data?

**No.** We never sell personal data to third parties for marketing. Data is shared only with subprocessors needed to operate the platform. See [Subprocessors](/en/subprocessors).

## What data does Bursa collect?

Identity (name, email), transaction data, learning progress, private Notes, and technical data (IP, device). Mentors also upload KYC documents. Full detail: [Privacy Policy](/en/policies).

## Can mentors see my private notes?

**No.** Your Notes/learning notes are private and are not shared with mentors, admins, or others.

## Does Bursa store credit-card numbers?

**No.** Payments are processed by a payment gateway (Midtrans/Xendit). Bursa does not store card numbers, CVV, or raw card data.

## How does Google login work?

We receive only email, display name, and public profile photo from Google. You can revoke access at any time via [Google Account Security](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## How do I delete my account?

Submit via [Data requests](/en/requests) or account settings. Some transaction data may be retained (anonymised) for tax duties.

## Where is data stored?

Cloud infrastructure (Vercel, PostgreSQL database) may be located outside Indonesia. We apply contractual controls under UU PDP. Detail: [Privacy Policy Article 9](/en/policies).

## Does Bursa use cookies?

Yes — essential cookies for login and platform function, plus optional analytics cookies (with consent). Detail: [Cookie Policy](/en/cookies).

## How long is data kept?

It varies by category — identity while the account is active, technical logs 90–180 days, transactions per tax duty. Full table: [Privacy Policy Article 6](/en/policies).

## Who can I contact with privacy questions?

Email [privacy@bursanalar.com](mailto:privacy@bursanalar.com) — response within 14 working days.
