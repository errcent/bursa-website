**If the Indonesian and English versions conflict, the Indonesian version governs.**

Bursa is a **trading-education platform**, not a licensed financial-services provider, broker, or personal investment adviser.

## UU PDP (Law 27/2022)

Bursa is controller. Rights via the [data-request form](https://privacy.bursanalar.com/en/requests). Breach SOP: 3×24 hours to authorities and users.

## POJK 6/2026

No guaranteed-profit claims. Education ≠ recommendation. Mentors are KYC’d; curriculum is reviewed. Platform-level buy/sell calls are out of scope.

## POJK 13/2025

Specific securities recommendations require an active investment-adviser licence. If a Signal feature ships, only licensed mentors.

## PCI

No PAN/CVV at Bursa. Gateway-hosted checkout (target SAQ A).

## What we do not claim

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are **not** held. PSE registration is planned before full public launch.
