## Commitments

- Privacy by design — minimisation, PII masking, learner Notes 100% private
- Layered security — TLS, RBAC, audit logging
- Regulatory posture — UU PDP, POJK 6/2026, PCI delegated to the payment gateway
- Honest transparency — no certificates we have not earned
