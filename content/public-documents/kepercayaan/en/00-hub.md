Welcome to Bursa’s Trust Center. We believe transparency is the foundation of trust — especially on a trading-education platform under regulatory scrutiny.

## Our commitments

- **Privacy by design** — data minimisation, PII masking, learner Notes 100% private
- **Layered security** — encryption in transit and at rest, RBAC, audit logs
- **Regulatory posture** — UU PDP, POJK 6/2026, PCI delegated to the payment gateway
- **Honest transparency** — we do not claim certifications we have not earned
