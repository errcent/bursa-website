_Effective: 22 July 2026_

**If the Indonesian and English versions conflict, the Indonesian version governs.**

Email **security@bursanalar.com** with description, reproduction steps, potential impact, and a PoC that does **not** touch real user data.

We acknowledge in 3 working days, assess in 7, and will not pursue researchers who follow this policy. A public bug-bounty program is **not** available yet.

## In scope

`*.bursanalar.com`, public APIs, authentication and authorisation.

## Out of scope

Social engineering, DoS, physical security, third-party vendor bugs (report to the vendor).

Do not access other users’ data, do not run destructive exploits, and do not publish before coordinated disclosure.
