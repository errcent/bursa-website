These Guidelines are part of the [Terms of Service](/en/terms). They exist to be **enforced**, not to decorate the footer.

## 1. Education, not tips

Do not give or solicit specific buy/sell/hold recommendations, promise profits, or post account screenshots as a “track record” to pull others into trades. Process, journaling, and frameworks are welcome.

## 2. One person, one account

Lifetime access after a single payment makes account sharing a dominant free-rider strategy. It is banned. We may cap devices and suspend without refund.

## 3. Respect

No harassment, hate, doxxing, or leaking others’ personal data. Learner Notes are private — even admins must not read them.

## 4. Honest reviews

Reviews come from verified purchases. Paid ratings, review-for-perk, and brigading are removed and may be sanctioned.

## 5. Don’t steal content

No recording, mirroring, or redistributing video. Circumventing technical protection is a serious breach.

## 6. Community (when enabled)

Use it to learn, not to funnel paid Telegram signal groups. Full community is not a year-one product promise; these rules still apply to comments, reviews, and any live communication features.

## 7. Enforcement ladder

1. Warning  
2. Mute / restrict posting  
3. Ban without refund for account sharing, pumping, harassment, or content theft  

We may skip steps for serious harm.

## 9. Changes

Operational updates may ship faster than the Terms. Indonesian remains governing.
