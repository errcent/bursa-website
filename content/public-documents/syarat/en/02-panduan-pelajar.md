If the Indonesian and English versions differ, **the Indonesian version governs.**

These Guidelines are part of the [Terms of Service](/en/terms). They exist to be **enforced**, not as polite suggestions. Without consequences, this text is cheap talk.

## 1. Education, not recommendations

This platform is education. In classes, comments, reviews, or community (if active) you **must not**:

- Give or solicit specific buy/sell/hold recommendations on instruments (stocks, crypto, forex, derivatives).
- Promise profit, “guaranteed gains”, or imitate illegal robot-trading schemes.
- Upload account screenshots as a “track record” to pull others into trades.

Analysis frameworks, journals, and process discussion are allowed. Stock tips are not.

## 2. One person, one account

Course access is **lifetime after a one-time payment**. Sharing an account is free-riding: one payment, many users. That is forbidden.

- Do not share passwords, sessions, or video links.
- Do not rent or sell access.
- Bursa may limit devices and disable accounts indicated as sharing **without a refund**.

## 3. Respect others

Harassment, hate speech, doxxing, threats, or disclosing another learner’s or mentor’s personal data is forbidden. Other people’s learning Notes are not yours — and admins must not read them either.

## 4. Honest reviews

Reviews come only from verified purchases. Buying/selling ratings, review-for-perk, or brigading is forbidden. That destroys the quality signal (Goodhart) and may lead to removal plus account sanctions.

## 5. Do not steal content

Recording, mirroring, or redistributing video/materials is forbidden. Technical protection (signed URLs, watermarks) exists for this; circumventing it is a serious breach.

## 6. Community (when enabled)

Community is a public good: easy to spam, hard to keep. If discussion spaces are live:

- Use them to learn, not as a funnel for paid signal/Telegram groups.
- Do not solicit personal data.
- Moderators may remove content without notice if it violates section 1 or 3.

A full community is **not** a year-one product promise; these Guidelines still apply to comments, reviews, and any communication features that exist.

## 7. Enforcement ladder (credible)

| Level | Example | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First minor violation | Warning |
| 2 | Repeat or moderate | Mute / restrict posting |
| 3 | Account sharing, pumping, harassment, video theft | Ban, without refund if abuse |

Bursa need not follow every step if the impact is serious (law, safety, minors).

## 8. Report

- Conduct: support@bursanalar.com
- Technical security: security@bursanalar.com
- Privacy: [data requests](https://privacy.bursanalar.com/en/requests)

Good-faith reports will not be met with retaliation. Repeated false reports may be sanctioned.

## 9. Changes

These Guidelines may be updated faster than the Terms because they are operational. Material changes will be announced on this page. The Indonesian version remains governing.
